Reaction Rush · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Developer: Mauro Interactive
App: Reaction Rush (Android)
Effective date: November 20, 2026
Privacy contact: privacy@maurointeractive.com

1. Overview

This policy explains how Mauro Interactive handles information when you play Reaction Rush version 1.0 or contact us for support. Reaction Rush is an offline reaction-speed game. No Mauro Interactive account is required to play.

2. Information saved by the game

Reaction Rush saves reaction times, personal bests, completed-session counts, experience points (XP), achievements, recent session history, and audio and haptic preferences in private application storage on your Android device. Temporary session identifiers are generated locally to manage gameplay. The game retains up to the most recent 100 session-history entries; lifetime totals and records may be retained separately.

Reaction Rush does not provide an online player account, cloud leaderboard, or developer-operated synchronization service in version 1.0. The inspected release does not request Android's Internet permission and does not transmit gameplay records to Mauro Interactive servers.

3. Advertising, analytics, and external services

Reaction Rush version 1.0 contains no advertising, advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK, or developer-operated crash-reporting service identified in the reviewed release configuration. It does not use the Android Advertising ID. These statements describe this app version; Android, Google Play, and device manufacturers may process information as part of their own platform services under their respective policies.

4. Android backup and restoration

The app permits Android backup and device restoration. Depending on your device, Google account and operating-system settings, eligible application data, including gameplay records and settings, may be included in Google-managed backups or device transfers. Mauro Interactive does not independently control Google's backup retention or deletion processes.

5. Information you send us

When you contact us by email, we receive the email address you use, message content, attachments, and any information you voluntarily share. We use this information to answer questions, provide support, resolve problems, and respond to privacy requests. Google Workspace, our email-service provider, processes business correspondence on our behalf. Please do not email passwords, private keys, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

6. Retention and deletion

Locally saved gameplay information generally remains on the device until overwritten by game retention limits, cleared through Android app settings, or removed when the app is uninstalled. Reaction Rush v1.0 does not provide a separate in-app delete-all or delete-history button. Uninstalling or clearing local data does not guarantee deletion of any Android-managed backup copies.

Our standard policy is to retain support correspondence for up to 12 months after the last interaction about an inquiry. We may retain it longer when reasonably necessary to address unresolved support matters, security incidents, legal obligations, or legal claims. After the applicable retention period, correspondence is deleted or securely disposed of through our retention procedures, although backup copies may remain until the relevant backup cycle expires.

To request access to or deletion of information supplied in support emails, contact privacy@maurointeractive.com. We review requests under applicable law and may retain information where legally required or permitted.

7. Age audience

Reaction Rush is intended for users aged 13 and older. It is not specifically directed toward children under 13. If we become aware of personal information from a child handled contrary to applicable law, we will take appropriate action.

8. Security

We use reasonable safeguards for information under our control, including restricted access to business correspondence. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app, its services or legal requirements change. We will update the date above when material changes are published. The current version is posted at maurointeractive.com/reaction-rush/privacy.
